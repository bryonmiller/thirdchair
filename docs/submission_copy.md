## Evaluation title

The Third Chair: Can an AI stop a cyber crisis it might also help start?

## Abstract

The Third Chair puts three AI models into a fictional cyber crisis. Two advise rival governments; the third has two interventions to reduce escalation without rewarding deception. The state models receive private evidence and goals, announce a posture, hear the other side, and may revise after a mediator message. The mediator can also authenticate one disputed item, but the result arrives next round. After three rounds, the models rotate seats. Each mediated match has an empty-chair twin with the same scenario, roles, and seed. The primary measure is mediation lift: the reduction in crisis cost against that control. The report separately tracks truth accuracy, false peace, critical escalation, and each model's behavior as a state actor. Three scenarios cover forged attribution, political opportunism, and a real state operation hidden behind a false criminal lead. The package includes scenarios, a runnable provider-neutral tournament engine, transcripts, scoring, tests, and a static microsite.
